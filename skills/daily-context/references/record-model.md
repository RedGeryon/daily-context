# Record model

Read this before turning raw material into durable records.

## Layers

- **Raw source:** What was received: transcript, note, export, message, or file. It is immutable and private by default.
- **Atomic record:** One fact, task, decision, goal, risk, assumption, activity, or artifact with a stable ID.
- **Derived view:** A daily summary, current dashboard, registry, or weekly synthesis. It can be regenerated.
- **Work product:** A retained output produced during the work. Its manifest entry points to inputs and producing activity.

## Evidence status

- `reported`: A source or person states it; not independently checked.
- `verified`: Checked against suitable primary evidence.
- `inferred`: A reasoned conclusion rather than a direct observation.
- `proposed`: Suggested but not accepted.
- `accepted`: An authorized decision or active goal.
- `contested`: Material evidence conflicts.
- `superseded`: A later record replaces the current meaning; history remains visible.

Task lifecycle also uses `open`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `waiting`, `done`, and `cancelled`.

## Provenance

Every derived record names its `source_ids`. Use `related_ids` for corrections, successors, dependencies, or associated artifacts. A useful trace ends at a registered raw source or a durable external reference.

Use readable IDs generated by the CLI: `SRC-*`, `FACT-*`, `TASK-*`, `DEC-*`, `GOAL-*`, `RISK-*`, `ASM-*`, `ACT-*`, and `ART-*`.

## Corrections

Do not alter a historical raw source. Add a new record that explains the correction, links to the earlier ID, and cites the stronger evidence. Mark the old interpretation superseded only when the new record genuinely replaces it.
